## The one scientific question

**What photographic flower-colour diversity is observed worldwide, what colour differences and recurrent directions occur within species, and what can these observations legitimately imply about moisture-dependent pigmentation trade-offs?**

The editorial order is **worldwide photographed colour diversity and moisture-associated composition → within-species colour-space and geographic structure → testable pigmentation benefit–cost hypothesis**. The high-depth H2 geometry is already measurable across **439** species under the original gate, but only **158** belong to the frozen prospective-confirmation cohort. This ordering does not change analytic chronology. The climate26 20,694-species and soil36 16,801-species panels are not nested antecedents of the 1,499-species high-depth sample or 158-species frozen prospective result. Different analytic grains must not be pooled to infer a shared causal mechanism. Pigment costs and morph-specific fitness were not measured.

## Inference chronology: what the manuscript may say

1. **Methodological result — reproducible distributional phenotype measurement.** Observer-disjoint high-depth estimates of visible four-state colour diversity and continuous nine-colour geometry, with a frozen prospective target, technical image-exposure audit, explicit unclassified observations and colour-construction-preserving nulls.
2. **Pattern result — recurrent achromatic–chromatic variation.** White–coloured colour-space displacement exceeds its construction-preserving structured null in prospective species. Local photographic colour mixtures and geographic differentiation are complementary aspects of the sampled distribution; the three-cohort local-depletion effect is robust under its original null and sensitivity rules.
3. **Ecological result — moisture–flower-colour correspondence.** Photographed white proportions have positive broad congeneric associations with BIO12 annual precipitation and atmospheric vapour pressure. The direction is based on the all-classified broad photographic scope and has explicitly bounded transport to stricter geographic, photo-quality and external-colour measurements. It motivates a water-mediated ecological payoff hypothesis.
4. **Balance falsification (retrospective).** White is 46.95% of originally classifiable global photographs, yet source-conditionally unusually uniform latitude values (P=.214) and a negative geographic-compensation mechanism (P=.997) are unsupported; photos alone cannot establish equilibrium. See S18 and the source-locked reporting-only result.
5. **Mechanistic hypothesis, not an already estimated fitness effect.** Pigmentation benefits, pigment investment, the pollination/visibility context, phenotypic plasticity and water stress are candidate trade-off components. A climatic difference in *relative reproductive fitness* and genotype-frequency dynamics are not identified by the current observation samples.

Results from exploratory alternate covariates, source limitations and null sensitivities are retained in Results/SI and frozen machine receipts; they are not removed to secure the positive framing.

## The one scientific question

**How can distributional photographic methods recover recurrent achromatic–chromatic flower-colour variation, and what is its global ecological relationship with moisture-related environments?**

The paper is organized **method → pattern → ecological mechanism hypothesis**, with three linked contributions: (1) a reproducible distributional measurement and inferential framework; (2) prospectively confirmed white–coloured phenotype-space structure, with local mixing and geographical differentiation as complementary pattern information; and (3) observed worldwide photographic floral colour–moisture associations, motivating a water-dependent benefit–cost balance of pigmentation. The latter is a **primary macroecological result**. The physiological trade-off itself remains a hypothesis; pigment costs and morph-specific fitness were not measured.

## Source and study-population firewall

- **High-depth prospective target**: 149,900 images / 1,499 species, with 100 photos per named source taxon. This is the only dataset used for three-cohort replicated local spatial organization and prospective H2. Its 42,111-species candidate metadata frame is NOT used to estimate prevalence.
- **Sparse macro panel**: 100,543 original photo opportunities / 42,111 named species; 45,953 classifiable four-state photo colours, with climate26 complete for 45,836 photos/25,028 species (**20,694 species/1,934 genera qualified for the main genus comparison**) and soil36 complete for 31,925 photos/20,786 species (**16,801 species/1,665 genera** qualified after the same rule). This is a different *sampling and analytic grain*, not another independent high-depth cohort. Climate locations remained iNaturalist; never call this a full externally sampled geography replication.
- These estimates are intentionally NOT pooled or counted as independent confirmations of the same null. Taxonomic/genus, photograph, plant morph, site and population are different statistical units.

## Inference chronology: what the manuscript may say

1. **Methodological result — reproducible distributional phenotype measurement.** Observer-disjoint high-depth estimates of visible four-state colour diversity and continuous nine-colour geometry, with a frozen prospective target, technical image-exposure audit, explicit unclassified observations and colour-construction-preserving nulls.
2. **Pattern result — recurrent achromatic–chromatic variation.** White–coloured colour-space displacement exceeds its construction-preserving structured null in prospective species. Local photographic colour mixtures and geographic differentiation are complementary aspects of the sampled distribution; the three-cohort local-depletion effect is robust under its original null and sensitivity rules.
3. **Ecological result — moisture–flower-colour correspondence.** Photographed white proportions have positive broad congeneric associations with BIO12 annual precipitation and atmospheric vapour pressure. The direction is based on the all-classified broad photographic scope and has explicitly bounded transport to stricter geographic, photo-quality and external-colour measurements. It motivates a water-mediated ecological payoff hypothesis.
4. **Mechanistic hypothesis, not an already estimated fitness effect.** Pigmentation benefits, pigment investment, the pollination/visibility context, phenotypic plasticity and water stress are candidate trade-off components. A climatic difference in *relative reproductive fitness* and genotype-frequency dynamics are not identified by the current observation samples.

Results from exploratory alternate covariates, source limitations and null sensitivities are retained in Results/SI and frozen machine receipts; they are not removed to secure the positive framing.

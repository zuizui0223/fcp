# Original 42,111-species FCP source: selection into the directly tipped LCVP phylogeny

The source-based local flower-colour/precipitation test originally used 872 source photographed species in fully within-250km genus×original equal-area cell groups and 1761 in within-500km groups. The real dated LCVP reference backbone directly contains only **342/872 (39.2%)** and **649/1761 (36.9%)** original photographed species, respectively. The remaining original taxa were kept out of the direct-tip phylogenetic model; genus/family synthetic placements are NOT equivalent to resolved dated species relationships.

The exploratory original dyadic source comparisons of photographed four-state mismatches under geography, heat, dated phylogenetic patristic distance and rain (PR #143; Actions 38008814183) produced no consistently positive added phylogenetic predictive gain. Rain beyond source dated phylogeny was positive under conditional genus-block resampling only in 500km parent / <=50km original photo groups, but NOT in the other three nested cohorts. The original source fine-geographical label-shuffle null across full 872/1761 cohorts did not support the broad local precipitation claim.

## Additional selection-into-tree audit

Compare the **identical original source species**, photo labels and environment metadata for all already selected source cohorts, separating true direct LCVP tips from original untipped photo species. Do not select any further taxa by flower colour or rainfall.

For each 250/500km source cohort, report:
- original source species, genuinely tipped and untipped counts, source genus/cell diversity and how many original genus names occur in both strata;
- conditional frequencies of the original four coarse photographed petal-colour states, white/yellow-orange/red-pink/blue-purple, in each phylogenetic-availability stratum, always retaining the source 872/1761 denominator;
- distribution of original real-photo site absolute latitude, elevation, and WorldClim BIO1/BIO5 temperature and BIO12/BIO15 rainfall/seasonality;
- **standardized mean differences**, direct-tree minus untipped group, for all six prespecified geographic/climate features; maximum absolute standardized mean difference and maximum source-photo colour-share difference.

The 0.10 absolute standardized-difference reference is descriptive imbalance diagnostics, not a p-value or adaptive mechanism. The directly-tipped source species were not chosen by photo flower colours during the taxon-to-LCVP matching, but literature/database phylogenetic sampling could still correlate with geographic or clade sampling and phenotype.

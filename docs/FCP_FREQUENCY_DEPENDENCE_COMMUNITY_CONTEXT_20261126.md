# Next biological test: when does flower-colour frequency dependence change sign?

**2026-11-26 — source screening and hypothesis contract, not a new empirical fitness fit or a systematic review.**

The completed FCP original-photo temporal test found no support for symmetric return towards equal WHITE/NONWHITE photo frequencies (166 species, 237 windows, statistic −0.007252, conditional one-sided p=0.64415). This is not a test of rare-morph invasion fitness, nor a rejection of asymmetric equilibria. The photographic observation frame has equal initial per-species photo quotas, so it is not a census of local conspecific or heterospecific flower abundance.

## Hypothesis to distinguish from the earlier universal rare-colour recovery idea

The sign of frequency-dependent *relative fitness* depends on the interaction context, not simply the focal species' rare colour. Separate the abundance of the focal colour morph within its own species (p), the abundance of relevant heterospecific colour signals (q), and the consequences of pollinator experience with rewards, compatibility and pollen transfer (R). A mechanism candidate is

log(W_A/W_B) = a(E) + b(R)(1−2p) + c(R)q,

where W_A and W_B are comparable actual reproductive-fitness measures, E describes the abiotic setting, and b(R)>0 is stabilizing conspecific frequency dependence in the simple two-phenotype model. c(R) may have different signs when shared colour facilitates legitimate pollination versus when similar colours increase incompatible pollen transfer or hybridization. This is an illustrative interaction hypothesis; parameters are not estimated. Generalizing across rewards alone is insufficient.

**Primary comparative test if suitable raw data exist:** within the same study system, fit morph-specific reproductive success across observed or manipulated starting focal-morph frequencies. Estimate the difference in per-capita relative reproductive success at different frequencies, and separately test whether heterospecific floral abundance or reward context explains a change in that frequency–fitness relationship. A direct frequency manipulation is stronger than an observational morph-frequency correlation. Do not pool visitation, pollinia removal, fruit set, seed viability and lifetime fitness as if they were the same endpoint. Do not infer independent study replicates from several endpoints of one experiment.

## Decision / next data

1. Preserve the verified negative symmetric convergence result as a separate exploratory-photo branch. Do not add a succession of sensitivity analyses to seek a favourable temporal statistic.
2. Acquire genuine plant-level reproductive-success data with local conspecific morph counts and, where available, heterospecific abundance; the Sisyrinchium dataset is a priority because it could permit explicit separation of the two frequencies if the raw tables contain both. Inspect the original tables and codebook before specifying a model. If they lack a focal frequency × focal morph comparison, classify the work only as a community-dependent selection test.
3. For the macroecological integration, compare the inferred conditions for distinct selection regimes with the already observed geographic photo-colour structure. Do not estimate community flower abundance or plant fitness from the 100-photos-per-taxon sampling design.

The original FCP main manuscript remains unchanged.

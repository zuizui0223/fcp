# White-transition mechanism screen v1 — 2026-09-25

Status: **exploratory full-coverage test frozen after a partial exact-cell screen was opened**.

This work is intentionally isolated from the submission-ready New Phytologist manuscript. It must not alter the frozen H2 claim, figures, thresholds, or submission guards.

## Biological question

The prospective H2 result establishes recurrent white-versus-nonwhite geometry, but it is sign-invariant and therefore does **not** establish an evolutionary transition direction.

This analysis asks a narrower ecological question:

> Within the same species, are measured white flowers repeatedly observed in different macroclimatic environments from pigmented flowers?

A repeated within-species association can identify an ecological **sorting/filtering candidate**. It cannot by itself show that pigmented flowers evolved into white flowers, nor that the environmental variable caused the transition.

## Primary contrast

The primary biological contrast is:

- white
- versus anthocyanic-colour proxy = red/pink + blue/purple

The rationale is mechanistic specificity. White-versus-all-nonwhite mixes anthocyanin-associated colours with yellow/orange states that can be generated by different pigment systems.

Two secondary contrasts are retained:

1. white versus all nonwhite;
2. white versus yellow/orange, used as a specificity comparator rather than as a negative control with guaranteed null expectation.

## Estimand

For each species and climate variable, require at least 5 white and 5 comparison rows after all filters.

Within each species, standardize the environmental variable across the retained white + comparison rows and calculate

[
d_i = \bar z_{white} - \bar z_{comparison}.
]

Positive values mean that white flowers occur toward higher values of the environmental variable within that species.

Species are the replication unit. The cross-species summary is the equal-weight mean of (d_i), with:

- 9,999 species bootstraps for the 95% interval;
- a two-sided sign test across nonzero species effects;
- Holm adjustment across the nine WorldClim variables within each contrast.

This prevents species with many photographs from dominating the cross-species conclusion.

## Outcome-blind low-dimensional environment

A PCA is fitted to the nine standardized WorldClim variables across all retained climate-evaluable third-cohort rows without using morph labels.

The first three PCs are then screened with the same species-equal effect definition, 9,999 species bootstraps and Holm adjustment across three axes.

PCA is used only to identify whether an integrated environmental axis is more stable than individual correlated BIO variables. Loadings must be reported.

## Interpretation rules

The following statements are allowed if supported:

- white records are repeatedly shifted toward warmer/cooler or wetter/drier macroclimates within species;
- the association is stronger for white-versus-anthocyanic than for white-versus-yellow/orange;
- a climate axis is a candidate ecological sorting filter for recurrent achromatic–chromatic variation.

The following are **not** established by this analysis:

- pigmented -> white evolutionary direction;
- anthocyanin loss as the molecular mechanism;
- causal climatic adaptation;
- pollinator causation;
- global prevalence of white-flower evolution.

Transition direction requires an independent phylogenetic ancestral-state analysis. Pollinator causation requires independently measured pollinator states/availability, ideally followed by a joint environment + pollinator model.

## Status relative to the partial screen

That partial screen suggested warmer and lower-annual-precipitation placement of white records relative to red/pink + blue/purple records, with little corresponding signal for white versus yellow/orange.

Therefore the present full-coverage WorldClim analysis is **not confirmatory**. It is an explicit full-coverage replication/diagnostic of an already-opened exploratory signal. A later independent-source or species-disjoint test is required for confirmation.

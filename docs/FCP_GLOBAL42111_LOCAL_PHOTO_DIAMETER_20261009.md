# FCP 42,111 original flower species: true-distance checks on local congeneric colour–environment prediction

The [fixed local genus×162-cell source-photograph comparison](../results/fcp_global42111_local_congeners_20261009/result.json) succeeded under synthetic tests and exact original 42,111 sample gates ([Actions 37893253874](https://github.com/zuizui0223/fcp/actions/runs/37893253874)). It found **6,290** climate-complete species from 1,280 original genus×cell groups (659 genera; 79 original cells) having >=3 distinct photographed congeneric taxa, and **4,564** soil+climate-complete species from 959 groups (524 genera; 68 original cells). Source-group membership was derived only from original genus and original public photo coordinates, NOT the photo-colour outcome.

The source-climate Brier improvement beyond geography/altitude for other local congeneric photographed species was +0.002221 on 6,290 taxa. Both temperature and precipitation blocks had positive separate increments after controlling the other. On 4,564 soil+climate-complete species the extra SoilGrids Brier increment was +0.002196 despite near-zero/no global increment in the previous larger taxon sample. This discrepancy is **not** necessarily an ecological contradiction because these assays target different taxa, cells, training conditions and spatial generalization.

## Fundamental remaining ambiguity: SAME original 162-cell is not a local site

Original photographic equal-area cells span about 20 degrees longitude and a varying sine-latitude strip width. Two different congeneric species in the same huge geographic cell can have photographs hundreds or thousands of kilometres apart. Therefore a "same-cell" association may still reflect long-distance biogeographic sorting. One source photo per species is not a phenotype population mean or genetic morph.

Precommit exactly **100, 250 and 500 km** maximal site-diameter limits; apply all, never pick the most positive threshold after seeing flower colours. For each original (genus, 162-cell) photo group, require >=3 distinct source taxa AND all member photographs to have a maximum pairwise GREAT-CIRCLE distance no greater than the given cap. Retain or discard the ENTIRE group, never select its nearest 3 species after inspecting colour outcomes. Evaluate both exact source climate-complete (18,413 originally classified species) and soil-complete (14,136 originally classified species) separately.

Groups containing source taxa spread across more than one cap are not permitted for that cap. Source group eligibility depends ONLY on original species IDs, nominal genus, original source geographic cell and validated individual original photo coordinates. Use the same train-only local genus×cell mean, deterministic genus-cell within-group photo-ID-based 5 folds, and fixed, compared source species across all base/geography/temp/precipitation/soil variants. Save **all three thresholds** with original source species/group/genus/cell counts, explicit HOLD if <300 support, and 999 bootstrap percentiles conditional on already held-out forecasts clustered at original cell, genus and local genus×cell.

A positive rain/soil increment with sufficiently narrow source-group photographed diameters is stronger descriptive evidence than a same-20°-cell comparison. However photo classifiability, within-group niche heterogeneity, taxon relatedness below genus, biotic variables, observer selection, pigment genotype and plant fitness remain unmeasured. One colour photograph is NEVER proof of genetically varying polymorphism, local selection or reproductive advantage.

The original New Phytologist 1,499 deeper photo cohorts and unopened independent 2,000+730 taxa remain separate. The workflow reads only previously checked source photograph/environment metadata from Actions 37861582446, no iNaturalist image downloads or raster re-extraction.

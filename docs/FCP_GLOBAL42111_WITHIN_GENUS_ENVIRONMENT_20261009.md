# Global FCP 42,111 source species: does climate–photo colour covariation survive WITHIN genus?

## Research distinction

The source-verified [42,111 species globally](https://github.com/zuizui0223/fcp/pull/138) show a **small between-species** geographic/altitude-adjusted climatic predictive gain for photographed four-state petal colour (14,136 taxa with all selected covariates, +0.00381 genus-heldout and +0.00416 photo-cell-heldout log-loss reduction) but no robust extra SoilGrids predictive increment. In contrast, the one-photo-per-endpoint **same-species cross-cell** study (3,182 colour/classifiable paired species) found no stable positive climatic difference effect on photo-colour mismatches, with both climate-gain intervals crossing zero.

These two claims leave a crucial and NONTRIVIAL middle ecological scale unresolved:

**Is apparent colour–climate sorting entirely due to WHICH GENERA live in each climate, or does a climate relationship persist among DIFFERENT SPECIES OF THE SAME GENUS?**

No prior genus-heldout global prediction answers this: holding out entire genera estimates transport to new genera, but does not remove among-genus differences from environmental predictor slopes. Nor does a same-species pair test capture within-genus species turnover.

## New source-fixed, leakage-safe assay

Inputs: immutable real-source 42,111 one-photograph-per-taxon classification with true-photo WorldClim 2.1 and SoilGrids values, generated by completed [Actions 37861582446](https://github.com/zuizui0223/fcp/actions/runs/37861582446). Read from its artifact without re-downloading any photographs, iNaturalist observations, climate or soils.

Two independently reported ORIGINAL complete case sets:

1. **Climate classifiable source** 18,413 original species: photographed four-state colour, true public source position, BIO1/BIO5/BIO12/BIO15, elevation. No soil completeness conditioning.
2. **Climate+soil classifiable source** 14,136 species: exactly the above plus non-missing source-site SoilGrids pH/SOC/N/clay/water. All 42,111 original taxa and 23,654 photo-unclassifiable taxa remain counted in a separate original denominator; never impute a missing petal colour.

### Fivefold original geo-cell heldout design

GroupKFold 5 splits by exact photographed original 162-cell equal-area cell. On each fold:

- Construct species' genus from first botanical-name token. Estimate per-genus four-colour mean and continuous environmental predictors' genus mean using **TRAINING original species only**. A held-out species enters estimation only if its genus has at least **two distinct training species**. All models evaluate the IDENTICAL eligible heldout photo IDs and 5-fold cell splits.
- Model family A: train-only genus category baseline.
- Model family B: genus baseline plus differences within genus along absolute latitude, longitude sine/cosine and true-source elevation.
- Model family C: plus within-genus climatic BIO1/BIO5/BIO12/BIO15 differences.
- Model D: plus within-genus soil pH/SOC/N/clay/water differences, tested **only on the identical 14,136-species soil-complete cohort**.

Use multivariate linear probability prediction of one original source photographed categorical outcome per species, genus-mean centring estimated on training species only; normalized/clipped predictions form valid 4-class probabilities. A fixed mild ridge penalty (10.0) guards near-collinear climate and geographic features; all families use same procedure and no hyperparameter selection after colour outcomes. Evaluate heldout **multiclass Brier score**, including train-only genus baseline. Positive incremental gain means lower heldout Brier in the more complex predictor.

Resample independent HELD-OUT ORIGINAL GRID CELLS, NOT individual images, 999 times for conditional bootstrap intervals of saved out-of-fold error difference. These intervals condition on the fixed realized 5-fold fit and are **not fully independent confirmation, a selective-inference-corrected test, or an algorithm-retraining bootstrap**.

Report the number of source species whose genus is train-estimable; source singleton genera cannot be used for this exact within-genus prediction. Do not switch sample size/feature definitions in response to results. The soil-complete test is a separately missingness-selected population and cannot replace all-classified climate evaluation.

## What any finding means

- **Climate improvement with genus fixed, spatial heldout, and intervals positive:** within-genus between-species colour/environment composition association beyond observed geographic gradients, NOT rapid within-species plasticity or climate-driven evolution.
- **No improvement:** the earlier weak global species-wide climate prediction does not generalize to the within-genus species-contrast assay; it may be driven by higher-level genus/lineage turnover or other sampling/morph/state factors. One source photograph and genus name are insufficient to *prove* genus turnover as the exclusive cause.
- **Soil increment only on some subset:** source-cover and land-mask selection may influence response. No claim of universal soil null.
- **Any model:** one photo per species and inferred local modelled soil are not genetic genotype, real mating population, pigment biosynthesis, pollinator-mediated selection or fitness.

The older 1,499 species high-depth original H1/H2/IBD/New Phytologist paper and independently future-selected 2,000+730 source outcomes are preserved without reinterpretation.

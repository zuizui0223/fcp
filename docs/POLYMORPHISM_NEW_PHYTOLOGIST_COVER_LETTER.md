We submit the Full Paper, **“Species-wide flower-colour variation is geographically partitioned across plant species,”** for consideration in *New Phytologist*.

### Question 1 — What hypotheses or questions does this work address?

When species vary in flower colour across their ranges, is that variation locally coexisting or geographically partitioned? Does colour turnover reflect geographic separation alone or also environmental differences, and do species repeatedly use common phenotype-space directions?

### Question 2 — How does this work advance our current understanding of plant science?

Across five species-disjoint cohorts and 447,500 actual photo-measurement terminals, nearby conspecific flowers were less colour-diverse than expected from each species' range-wide composition (1,761 spatially eligible species; pooled p = 0.005). Technical falsification tests in the original three cohorts bounded this broad pattern without identifying its ecological cause.

### Question 3 — Why is this work important and timely?

A species-wide ITV value can conceal where variation resides. We show that flower-colour diversity is distributed among localities, creating spatial structure on which selection, gene flow, drift or plasticity can act. This moves comparative ITV from measuring variation amount to asking how variation is geographically maintained.

## Key result and evidential chronology

The study begins from an outcome-blind **42,111-species iNaturalist opportunity frame**. A high-depth resource contains **500 discovery + 500 species-disjoint validation species and 100,000 photographs**; a later cohort adds **499 newly sampled species and 49,900 photographs** after the achromatic–chromatic target was frozen.

The central ecological result was first obtained in the original three species sets and subsequently transported, without estimator changes, to another **2,976** newly sampled species across two post-freeze waves (498 plus 2,478). At the fixed 50-km scale, mean species-wide minus local four-state pair diversity was **0.02053** in discovery, **0.01867** in validation and **0.01468** in the third cohort; a composition-preserving matched null gave **p = 0.005 in all three**. The effect persisted when same-observer photo pairs were excluded, when every white observation was removed, and when continuous nine-colour dissimilarity replaced the coarse states. The same local depletion was also detected at 25, 100 and 250 km in the original study. The genuine fifth-wave 2,478-species original-photo expansion added **1,020** further taxa meeting the 50-km local-pair gate (depletion **0.01760**, p = **0.005**). All five cohorts together comprise **4,475 authentically measured species / 447,500 original photo terminals**, with **1,761** spatially eligible taxa and pooled 50-km depletion **0.01761** (p = **0.005**).

Continuous colour turnover contained both geographic and environmental structure. Geographic distance remained associated with colour after BIO5 difference was removed (mean partial rho = **0.02760**, **0.02414**, **0.02225** across the three cohorts), while BIO5 difference retained a smaller association after geography was removed (**0.00925**, **0.00826**, **0.01019**). We describe the latter only as a phenotypic IBE-like candidate because matched-background and strict same-observer controls do not confirm it as a flower-specific, observer-independent environmental response.

A complementary prospectively protected result concerns phenotype space. The third cohort contained **499 species and 49,900 photographs**, of which **377** passed the measurement-support gate. After freezing the achromatic–chromatic target, **158** primary-tier species yielded W = **0.517** versus a structured-null median of **0.457** (p = **0.001**); the strict tier yielded W = **0.533** (p = **0.001**). This remains the earlier untouched prospective test, not a pooled same-source replication. On the separate completed five-cohort source, the unchanged fixed white-axis W was **0.51879** (structured-null median **0.45537**; n=1,223; p=0.001), and observer-disjoint D reliability had median rho **0.80354**. Neither result identifies one universal adaptive cause.

## Scope boundary

The distributed-polymorphism analyses are explicitly post hoc, although the unchanged 50-km depletion repeats across **five** species-disjoint same-provider cohorts. The original three, not all five, received the complete observer/nonwhite/continuous-colour falsification battery. They establish **geographic partitioning of phenotype variation**, not local adaptation. The photographs do not measure fitness, neutral population structure, reciprocal-transplant performance or gene flow, so spatially varying selection cannot be separated from drift, dispersal limitation, colonisation history or phenotypic plasticity.

The prospective achromatic–chromatic test remains species-disjoint but within the **same iNaturalist opportunity universe** and measurement system; it is **not an independent-source replication**. The coarse white state is exposure-coupled rather than artifact-cleared: the direct near-clip control gave OR = **1.444**, and the frozen executable validity state remained **INDETERMINATE** after high-clip exclusion narrowly missed its vector-retention gate. The BIO5 IBE-like residual is technically bounded and is not used as causal evidence.

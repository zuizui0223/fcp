# FCP white-state heat × pigment-network mechanism ledger — 2026-09-25

## Current mechanistic model

`
genetic / regulatory perturbation ┐
                                  ├─> pigment-network flux -> pale / achromatic floral endpoint
thermal perturbation -------------┘
`

The strongest current evidence is **pathway-level convergence**, not a universal temperature main effect and not a universal nonwhite -> white evolutionary direction.

## Evidence ledger

### A. Recurrent phenotype-space axis — supported

The frozen FCP H2 result establishes recurrent white-versus-nonwhite achromatic–chromatic geometry beyond the coarse-state-preserving structured null.

This is a phenotype-space result. It does not identify mechanism or evolutionary direction.

### B. Recurrent nonwhite -> white evolutionary direction — not supported

OpenTree endpoint analysis:
- 196 tips;
- 116 nonwhite-dominant, 80 white-dominant;
- 100/100 topology resolutions completed;
- median q(nonwhite->white) / q(white->nonwhite) = 0.7007;
- fraction with ratio > 1 = 0.

Frozen verdict:
`DIRECTIONAL_ASYMMETRY_NOT_SUPPORTED_UNDER_THIS_TEST`.

Therefore the recurrent axis must not be described as repeated confirmed pigment loss through evolutionary time.

### C. Universal broad environmental driver — not supported

Third cohort alone:
- BIO5 white association passed its prospective gate.

Species-disjoint legacy replication:
- discovery: median within-species BIO5 contrast -0.0068 SD, Wilcoxon p=0.743;
- reserve: +0.0662 SD, Wilcoxon p=0.0541;
- frozen two-cohort verdict: `LEGACY_BIO5_WHITE_REPLICATION_NOT_SUPPORTED_UNDER_THIS_TEST`.

Seasonal-heat follow-up:
- broad/species-stratified association positive;
- same-cell local p=0.054995, failing the prespecified full gate.

Thus temperature is not supported as one universal FCP ecological cause.

### D. Universal pollinator driver — not supported

Tested common predictors did not pass:
- Sphingidae;
- Lepidoptera fraction;
- effort-adjusted bee breadth;
- local bee richness;
- local Bombus fraction.

Pollinators remain valid system-specific maintenance/selection mechanisms.

### E. Natural white/pigmented molecular convergence — supported as systematic mechanistic synthesis

At least five natural systems independently map white / strongly depigmented floral states to reduced, blocked or rerouted anthocyanin/flavonoid-network output:

1. *Gymnadenia rhellicani* — R2R3-MYB -> ANS / cyanidin;
2. *Ipomoea purpurea* — CHS loss and bHLH regulatory lesions;
3. *Parrya nudicaulis* — petal-specific CHS downregulation / cis-regulatory control;
4. *Pleione limprichtii* — FLS / ANS / UFGT and MBW-network expression;
5. *Silene littorea* — strong F3H downregulation with candidate Myb1a regulation.

The convergence is at the network/output level, not one universal gene.

*Abronia fragrans* remains a betalain counterexample to universal anthocyanin causation.

### F. Same-species natural heat × pigment bridge — present

*Parrya nudicaulis*:
- white-flower frequency increases with growing-season temperature across natural populations;
- the same white phenotype maps to early anthocyanin-pathway regulatory reduction near CHS.

This is observational ecology plus molecular evidence, not a randomized temperature experiment.

### G. Direct within-species thermal dose on floral anthocyanin — strong

*Moricandia arvensis* public Source Data:

Primary mild-summer -> hotter-summer sequence under the same stated 16/8 h summer photoperiod:
- 15 individuals;
- mean cyanidin 1.893 -> 0.531;
- hot/mild = 0.281;
- ~71.9% reduction;
- 13/15 individuals decrease;
- paired Wilcoxon p=0.000854.

Reversal:
- 14 individuals mild-summer -> spring return;
- cyanidin 1.236 -> 4.708;
- 12/14 increase;
- p=0.000610.

Spring -> hot:
- 14/14 decrease;
- 5.690 -> 0.179;
- one-sided p=6.10e-5.

This is the strongest direct temperature-to-pigment evidence in the current mechanism line.

### H. Summer-white transcriptome overlaps FCP natural-white nodes — strong compatibility

Moricandia significant-DEG table, summer-white relative to spring-lilac:

All four FCP node classes represented in the significant-DEG table are lower:
- MYB90: logFC -4.056, FDR 4.81e-11;
- CHS: logFC -1.293, FDR 0.034;
- U75C1/U78D2 class: median logFC -1.5315, all listed FDR < 0.05;
- TTG1/WD40: logFC -1.411, FDR 0.0279.

Additional pathway context:
- PAL down;
- 4CL down;
- DFR down.

F3H, ANS, FLS and bHLH are absent from the significant-DEG table; absence is not evidence of no change.

### I. Anthocyanin-vs-flavonol branch specificity — formal gate not estimable; descriptive pattern strong

Frozen primary statistic:
`log(cyanidin_hot/cyanidin_mild) - log(flavonol_hot/flavonol_mild)`.

Two measured cyanidin individual-period means equal zero, so the log-ratio primary is undefined. No pseudocount was introduced.

Frozen verdict:
`ANTHOCYANIN_BRANCH_SPECIFICITY_NOT_SUPPORTED_UNDER_THIS_TEST`.

Descriptive trait-level pattern:
- cyanidin: 1.893 -> 0.531; ~71.9% lower; 13/15 decrease; p=0.000854;
- UV-absorbing flavonols: 58.44 -> 57.18; ~2.1% lower; 8/15 decrease, 7/15 increase; p=0.679.

This is consistent with anthocyanin-selective suppression / flavonoid rerouting rather than a uniform collapse of flavonoid metabolism, but the formal branch-specificity gate is not claimed as supported.

## Current interpretation

The data are most coherent under a **many-to-one pigment-network accessibility model**:

1. multiple genetic/regulatory lesions can lower pigment-network output and reach a white/pale endpoint;
2. temperature can perturb overlapping nodes of the same network in at least some systems;
3. the magnitude and even sign of thermal effects are genotype-, developmental-stage-, light- and metabolic-context dependent;
4. therefore a recurrent achromatic phenotype axis does not require a universal cross-species BIO5 coefficient.

## Strongest testable next hypothesis

The next decisive experiment is not another global climate regression.

It is:

`
natural colour genotype / morph × temperature
`

with repeated measures of:
- reflectance / colour space;
- anthocyanin concentration;
- flavonol allocation;
- CHS, F3H, DFR, ANS, UFGT;
- MYB / bHLH / WD40 regulators.

Primary estimand:

`
genotype_or_morph × temperature interaction
`

Prediction:
pigmented genotypes/morphs carrying heat-sensitive pathway configurations should move disproportionately toward the achromatic endpoint under heat, whereas already white / pigment-floor morphs should show smaller chromatic reaction norms.

## Claim ceiling

Supported:
- recurrent achromatic–chromatic phenotype geometry;
- repeated natural molecular convergence on pigment-network output;
- direct within-species thermal suppression of anthocyanin in Moricandia;
- overlap between Moricandia summer-white DEG nodes and FCP natural-white molecular nodes.

Not supported:

- universal pollinator cause;
- recurrent nonwhite -> white transition asymmetry;
- one universal white-flower gene;
- formal anthocyanin-vs-flavonol branch-specificity gate.

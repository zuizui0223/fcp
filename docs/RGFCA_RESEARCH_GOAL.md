# RGFCA ecological signal discovery and independent validation

User-directed research objective, 2026-09-07 (Japan).

## Objective

Make RGFCA the FCP mainline and obtain an ecologically interpretable observed
signal that withstands independent validation, then complete a submission-ready
manuscript, figures, reproducibility bundle and CI-verified PR.

The six-species Chapter 1 analysis, 34-species literature comparison and earlier
small pilots are legacy evidence. Preserve their original data, results and
provenance; they are not the empirical foundation of the new RGFCA claim.

## Starting evidence

The current RGFCA experiment measured 50,000 photographs from 500 species.
Its eligible frame contains 21,424 classifiable photographs from 369 species.
The repeated maps, complete-vector species-conditioned randomizations,
environmental fields, measurement audits and synthetic identifiability work are
reusable methodological assets. Their completed failures and non-support
decisions remain part of the record.

The first pending analysis is the frozen 369-species geographic-distance versus
colour-dissimilarity omnibus. Its last CI attempt stopped at module import,
before computing species permutation outcomes. Restore the execution environment
without changing its fixed estimand, denominator or randomization rules.

## Research route

1. Complete the existing RGFCA-only spatial omnibus and assess the effect,
   uncertainty, measurement limitations and species-level information.
2. Use that evidence and existing power diagnostics to choose bounded exploratory
   questions about spatial organization, environmental response, pollinator
   context or community assembly. Record each question and analysis family
   before execution, including known outcomes that motivated it.
3. Retain all tested outcomes and account for multiplicity. Do not interpret
   repeated maps of the same photographs as independent biological replication.
4. Test promising candidates using independent observations or a genuinely unused
   and prospectively sealed species/region holdout. Data already used in earlier
   exploratory analyses cannot be relabelled as independent validation.
5. Deliver an ecological interpretation with its scope and limits, species-free
   atlas figures, species-conditioned inference, manuscript, reproducibility
   bundle and CI-complete PR.

## Success criterion

A signal observed only after exploring the same dataset is a candidate, not the
completed objective. Completion requires an ecologically interpretable observed
effect with credible measurement, uncertainty and independent validation, plus
the publication artifacts above. Synthetic recovery rates establish properties
of a method under simulated conditions, not a biological discovery.

The research direction may change with evidence. Frozen results are not changed
to manufacture support; null, non-evaluable and technical failure outcomes stay
visible. This document does not authorize fees, external submissions or reuse of
protected data outside the research contracts.

**Date:** 21 October 2026. **Status:** draft figure reorganization; old submitted-source figures still exist and are not silently claimed as the new publication panels. This document serves the updated manuscript on the NP global-evolutionary-synthesis branch.

## Main paper in one sentence

Across 4,475 taxa, a prospectively confirmed achromatic–chromatic phenotype direction coexists with modest, heterogeneous geographic placement and a small, poorly transferable average climate association. Recurrence in visible phenotype space cannot alone identify shared molecular evolution, local selection or heritable polymorphism maintenance.

## No duplication of hypotheses as a set of 5 independent results

The five evolutionary hypotheses H1–H5 are NOT the same as the historical frozen pipeline labels H1 reliability, H2 white direction and H3a/H3b secondary tests. Their mapping is:

- Evolution H1 (phenotype-space accessibility): **direct prospective test of observed direction**; molecular origin untested.
- Evolution H2 (history): existing IBD-like photo associations only; **no independent genetic/history evidence** in global atlas.
- Evolution H3 (current environment): signed BIO5 association and environmental distance conditional correlations; no replicated selection or fitness. Unseen spatial prediction often worsens.
- Evolution H4 (maintenance): strict local coexistence test p=.300 and 34 literature C/S species with only 5 matches in photo 50-km analysis, S-only matches 0; **no global comparison of heritable C vs S states**.
- Evolution H5 (molecular modularity): external published PAL/WAL/pigment expression contrasts only; **no 447,500-photo individual-molecular linkage**.

# One New Phytologist study: within-species flower-colour variation and polymorphism

**14 October 2026 · Reporting-only editorial plan.** The frozen original high-depth manuscript and numeric H1/H2 decisions remain the scientific authorities. This plan concerns **one** within-species paper and deliberately does not merge the distinct between-species global moisture atlas now maintained in `zuizui0223/mois`.

## Focus and principal estimand

**Primary biological question:** Does independently sampled within-species photographed flower-colour variation repeatedly occupy the same visible achromatic–chromatic direction across plants, beyond the structured colour-class construction baseline?

**Second ecological question:** Does species-wide photographed flower-colour diversity consist only of freely mixed local colours, or is it partly allocated among observed geographic localities?

A natural reproductive population's **genetically inherited morph polymorphism** is a potential ecological explanation, not identical to the photographed nine-colour distributions or four-category labels measured here. No population genotype, pigment chemistry, colour-dependent fitness, or morph-frequency demographic time series were obtained.

## Single article's method → pattern → ecological hypotheses

1. **Methodological result:** derive location-blind per-species four-colour distributions and continuous nine-palette phenotypes from high-depth iNaturalist images. Observer-disjoint validation rho (median) **0.789102993**, fresh-image transport on 136 overlapping taxa rho **0.9681**, data acquisition and technical ambiguity tracked separately. D estimates are sample-based; high-rank correlation does not validate a genetic colour morph.
2. **Recurrent pattern:** same white-versus-nonwhite colour-space axis was identified in original discovery/validation species and prospectively tested in a new source subset. Original primary-tier eligible photo-vector species: discovery **152** + validation **129** + prospective **158** = **439 source-eligible species in all three species-disjoint cohorts**, but **439 is descriptive, not one pooled prospective confirmatory test**. Third-cohort frozen W = **0.517245746** versus constructed-null median **0.457142815**, P=**0.001**; strict prospective sensitivity W = **0.532928** among 86 eligible species, P=**0.001**. Prospective H2 frozen verdict remains **H2_PROSPECTIVE_WHITE_AXIS_CONFIRMED**.
3. **Geographic ecological pattern:** at the previously fixed posthoc 50 km photographic locality radius, relative local pairwise colour diversity was depleted by **0.020529 /0.018673 /0.014685** in discovery (**166** eligible species), validation (**181**) and third (**204**), each exact species-composition matched-null P = **0.005**. 25, 100, 250 km, no-white, same-observer and continuous-colour checks retain the directional phenomenon. This is not a local allele-frequency or adaptation measurement.
4. **Ecological processes to test:** the IBD-like geographical colour component exceeds the smaller photographic BIO5-associated phenotypic IBE-like residual; strict image/observer controls and cross-cohort signed BIO5 transport do not establish a shared thermally selected white morph. Developmental anthocyanin regulation, pollination, drift, migration, ecological water stress and morph-dependent costs are plausible **hypotheses**. Genetic balancing/frequency dependence and morph-specific fitness are **not inferred**.

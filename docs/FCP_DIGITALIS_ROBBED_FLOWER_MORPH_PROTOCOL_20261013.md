# Digitalis: pre-outcome protocol for a source-authenticated field flower-robbery comparison

2026-10-13 JST. **Exploratory, source-exposed and retrospectively specified after reviewing source schema, not externally preregistered.** Freeze this document BEFORE computing morph-group robbed-flower means, test statistics, or p values. This is a second species, not a repeated-year test like Linanthus.

## Ecological question
Does the source field variable `Robbed_flower` differ between **violet and pink flowers** within each of five *Digitalis purpurea* survey populations, after respecting repeated flowers on the same recorded **individual/patch**? Robbed=yes is an **observation flag**, not an independently verified pollinator, antagonist species or fitness outcome. Do not infer flower-morph population frequency from the selected measurement sample.

## Main endpoint and analysis
- For each eligible grouped individual/patch, count `Robbed_flower=='yes'` and `=='no'` among inspected flowers and use **plant/patch equal-weighted fraction** `r_i=yes/(yes+no)`, requiring ≥1 valid inspected flower. Do not map `eaten`, `damaged` or NA to `no`.
- Within site, compute the difference of equal-plant averages **violet minus pink**; the **primary estimate is mean across the 5 sites with each site one vote**. State every site contrast and sample sizes, including nonconforming H_2 in a separate descriptive analysis. Do not include white in the main contrast because the surveyed Bolivia site GPS-160 lacks a white category.
- Require ≥5 eligible colour-unambiguous plant/patch groups in EACH of violet and pink at every one of the 5 primary sites, otherwise `HOLD_INSUFFICIENT_MORPH_SUPPORT` (do not silently drop weak sites). All flower observations belonging to a plant must remain together.
- For a conditional no-association reference distribution, permute complete **plant/patch colour labels within the same site**, preserving site-morph counts, keep plant outcome fractions intact; **9,999 replicates**, seed **2026101301**; two-sided plus-one p. The test is conditional on observed sampled plant outcome and label exchangeability, **not causal selection**, and sites are fixed rather than a representative random population sample. Compute 1,999 within-site×morph plant/patch bootstraps, seed **2026101302**, with 95% percentile interval. Clearly state fixed-site conditional inferential scope.
- **Temporal ascertainment guard**: calculate colour-overlap by original `Date` within every site. Site-day strata with ≥2 grouped units of both colours may be subjected to the same label permutation within site-day strata. If <3 primary sites contribute or <50 plants are jointly represented, HOLD date-conditional inference as unsupported; otherwise show it as a descriptive sensitivity (no selective promotion). Mixed dates can confound site-only associations.
- **Secondary endpoints**: plant-equal mean `Length_flower` (mm) for violet/pink within site, with independent 1,999 within-site permutations (seed **2026101303**), and three-colour site-wise robbed fractions (descriptive). If primary and secondary differ in direction, do not swap primary endpoints. After discovering color-specific means, do not alter exclusion, weights, or hypotheses. Check whether `Robbed_flower` has observational meaning established in original field protocol; if unclear, call it a column label only.
- Include sample-quality ledger: all 1,323 source flower rows; 2023/2024 counts, H_2 exclusion, number of duplicate plant groups, any conflicting colour group, missing robbed flags, date overlap, per site counts, flower count per plant, two-country observational scope. Keep every exclusion auditable.

## Interpretation and integration boundaries
A positive conditional association could indicate flower phenotype correlates with *observed robbing flags* within selected plants, but may reflect sampling date, floral abundance, patch definition, observation effort, site and observer effects; colour not assigned at random. A null cannot establish no antagonistic or pollinator selection. The sampled plants are not population censuses; source authors already found population-dependent pollinator visitation and no consistent morph-related floral trait effects. Never claim a new ecological mechanism solely from correlations.

Require original-byte MD5+SHA verification, tests on synthetic repeated flowers and conflicting labels, a separate independent aggregation check, same-site sampling-label null, sensitivity date-overlap ledger, and honest negative findings.

# RGFCA old34 transfer closure — 2026-09-10

## Decision

Close the historical 34-species C/S hypothesis-transfer line after Steps 7C–7F. Do not add post-hoc thresholds, alternative climate products, state redefinitions, or significance-rescue analyses.

## What transferred successfully

The historical estimands and testing logic were transferred prospectively to the photo-derived RGFCA organization system:

- H2 sampled-coordinate fragmentation;
- H9 local morph-specific photo-date partitioning proxy;
- H1a mean occupied BIO4;
- H1b mean occupied BIO15;
- H3a spatial environmental turnover;
- H3b regional climatic centroid separation;
- H3c regional climatic overlap;
- H0 rarefied total PC1–PC3 climatic hypervolume as a negative-control diagnostic.

Discovery and reserve were kept independent.

## What failed to recur

No predeclared directional historical mechanism recurred in both tranches.

- H2 fragmentation: unsupported in both tranches.
- H9: cross-state contrast not estimable because the eligible set lacked a C*=0 comparison group; no threshold rescue was attempted.
- H1a/H1b/H3a/H3b/H3c: none met the two-tranche recurrence gate.

Therefore RGFCA does not support carrying the old34 climatic-mechanism story forward as a global explanation of photo-derived C*/S* organization.

## The H0 anomaly and why it is not promoted

Step 7D found much larger rarefied total climatic hypervolume in pure S* than pure C* in both tranches.

Step 7E showed that geographic extent is strongly associated with both H0 and S* state, but extent adjustment did not remove the association.

Step 7F then tested the full Step-7A classifier under within-species morph-label exchangeability. Coordinates, observer IDs, morph counts, and real frozen hypervolume values were held fixed; morph labels alone were shuffled. The same C*/S* classifier was rerun in 999 null worlds.

The observed H0 contrasts were ordinary under this classifier-null:

- discovery observed 9.3718; classifier-null median 9.4003; one-sided p=0.502543;
- reserve observed 9.6618; classifier-null median 11.7865; one-sided p=0.588735.

Thus the H0 association can be generated by the state-construction/sampling geometry itself and is not promoted as an ecological niche-breadth result.

## What remains valid from the RGFCA extension

The defensible outputs are methodological/structural rather than a recovered old34 mechanism:

1. photo-derived organization is not binary: local co-occurrence only, spatial segregation only, both, and unresolved states all occur;
2. those state frequencies recur qualitatively across the independent discovery and reserve tranches;
3. the atlas rarefaction result provides an empirical design floor for recovering the frozen global photo-colour summaries under the strict Step-7B criteria;
4. old34 mechanism transfer was actually capable of failing under predeclared gates;
5. the strongest apparent post-transfer environmental signal was itself rejected by a classifier-null diagnostic.

## Main-line consequence

Return the RGFCA main line to the atlas-level question: which global colour structures are reproducible across species and repeated photo samples, and which survive null models that reproduce observation density, species composition, and state-construction geometry?

Do not use old34 C/S mechanism hypotheses as the organizing narrative for the RGFCA paper unless a genuinely new, prospectively defined evidence source addresses a different estimand.

H8 remains unopened and is not part of this closure because its drought/UV/soil/extreme-stress evidence family was never instantiated in the RGFCA transfer.

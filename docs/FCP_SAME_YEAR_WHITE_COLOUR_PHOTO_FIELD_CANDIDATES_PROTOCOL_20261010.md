# Source-frozen screening of white/coloured floral-photo co-occurrence for field experiments

Date: 2026-10-10. This is an **observational photograph-level candidate screen**, not proof of true co-occurring botanical morphs or genetic polymorphism.

## What changes relative to the earlier 50:50 statement?

The species-equal source global 1165-species screen gave about 48.34% photo-white. This is not a within-population equilibrium. First test whether specific source high-depth species have both previously automatically scored photo-white and nonwhite **at a similar time and geographic place**.

Three original, mutually distinct photo-deep cohorts: original archived discovery/validation measured files from commit 5142f7951af0dde5364bb047a566d67e8c479e51 and third from 7e538e5c51c05a7cc47b2fcf53eea92634c8a863. Hard-gate the three SHA256s in the actual source-replay workflow and original >=40 classifiable species numbers 369/363/377 (total **1109 high-depth species**). No new image classifier labels.

## Frozen pre-outcome case definitions

1. Each original photo has a public photo-ID and automatically classified four-state visible flower-colour (white, yellow/orange, red/pink, blue/purple), original observer ID, observed date, and public latitude/longitude. Recode white vs all three coloured states **only for candidate sourcing**, not as evolutionary genotypes.
2. **Primary time window**: same named species, *same original year AND month*. Distinct years with same calendar month separately reported as **weaker secondary temporal opportunity**, never evidence for actual contemporaneous co-occurrence.
3. **Exact pair**: two original photos of the same species, one photo-white and one photo-nonwhite, photographed by **two different nonmissing observers**, max observed photographed coordinate distance 10km. Also fix 25/50km secondary diameters. This is only a candidate observation, not two different biological plants.
4. **Conservative four-photo corroboration**: two photo-white images and two photo-colour images, **four different observer IDs**, all in the same year+month and all within **10km maximum observed pair diameter**. For computational clarity, identify all four around one source photo at <=5km from that anchor. That guarantees <=10km diameter but is not geometrically exhaustive: a real quadruple could exist without a photographed central anchor. Test additional 25/50km diameter and cross-year same-calendar-month variants. Do **not** call failing the anchored witness biological monomorphism.
5. Reproducibly report all 6 combinations (10/25/50km diameters × same-year-month / same-calendar-month across years) separately, and original n species with >=1 each photo-colour and >=5 each source photo colours. Counts by discovery/validation/third are explicitly reported, not selected for positive-only storytelling.
6. Internal original-species ledger retains per-species summary counts and temporal/geographic witness feasibility **without listing any original photo IDs, exact coordinates, original species-panel confidential blind audit case IDs, or per-photo machine colour labels**. Only candidate names and aggregate opportunities are allowed and are **not handed to 405-case human reviewers**.

## Empirical risk to be confronted

A single source photo can show multiple plants, flowers from a different taxon, a bract vs corolla, artificial planting, or a different measurement organ. Public coordinate 10km is not one freely mating natural population. Original source labels exhibit white photographic exposure sensitivity, and stage-1 405-photo expert colour review remains unperformed. The candidate witness audit is **triage for subsequent herbarium and in-situ voucher verification**; no confirmed sympatric genetically segregating morph species is claimed.

A meaningful shortlist must independently check (a) documented natural co-occurring colour morphs, (b) actual flower organ spectral/chemical class, (c) free-living field access/flowering dates, (d) multiple distinct individual plants and genetic families, (e) ideally congeneric close relatives of opposing colours for between-species replication, (f) at least two different environmental/photoperiod conditions, and (g) measuring seed/pollen fitness and insect visitation feasible. Original photo mixed states alone cannot satisfy these gates.

### Success/hold decision

- A positive photo **pair** provides one field reconnaissance target, not evidence of population polymorphism.
- A positive photo **four-witness** provides a higher-priority source image/observer audit target, not a validated genotype.
- If zero such witnesses exist, report genuine **insufficient documentary support in sampled source photos**, not morphological absence in nature. No forced relaxation of original 10km/same-year-month criterion to salvage the hypothesis.
- Ranking is explicitly post hoc and should not be used as a confirmatory source of interspecific evolution.

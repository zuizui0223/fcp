# FCP — one biological argument across unequal estimable species sets
Date: 2026-11-19. Status: source-grounded synthesis and prespecified next-analysis contract; **not** a newly fitted result.

## Decision
**Do not force every estimand into one 1,070-species denominator.** Keep the fixed 4,475-species photographic registry, estimate each scientific quantity in its eligible risk/opportunity set, and explicitly measure whether selection into each set changes the conclusion. The narrative is about **organization of photographed within-species colour variation**, not about prevalence of genetic flower-colour polymorphism.

The 447,500 original photos represent 4,475 taxa, with 227,107 originally four-colour-classifiable and 220,393 unclassified. All photo cohorts use the same platform and measurement procedure.

## Scientific progression, with separate estimands
1. **Observable diversity and phenotype direction:** 2,400/4,475 taxa have at least five original photographs in at least two coarse classes; this is an observation threshold, not a true biological polymorphism prevalence. In the all-4,475 P1 analysis the white–chromatic direction W=0.478872 vs matched null 0.432039 (199-world p=0.005). W is photographic phenotype-space geometry, not pigment synthesis cost.
2. **Spatial allocation conditional on opportunity:** 3,600 taxa have a classified same-species pair within 50 km; 50-km categorical global-minus-local mismatch +0.016116 (p=0.005). The strict gate (>=40 classifiable photos and >=30 local pairs) has 1,761 taxa and +0.017614902 (p=0.005). Nine-palette month-and-coarse-category conditional 50-km JSD excess +0.0051116 (genus 95% +0.0042504 to +0.0059493, 999 worlds, Holm across 15 comparisons p=0.015). This is within-species photographic geography, not inherited-morph local adaptation.
3. **Environment as a candidate correlate, not a cause:** Univariate BIO5/Firth contrast 4,112 taxa: +0.021888 log odds per degree C, genus 95% +0.00846 to +0.03489. A **different** estimand, shared standardized thermal-level photographic white-fraction slope, has 2,837 taxa and +0.008209 fraction per within-species thermal-index unit, with positive mean in both observer-disjoint folds (2,403 taxa +0.012879; 2,430 taxa +0.005747). Only 54.2% of individual species' slopes are positive. The 1,919 taxon paired-fold correlation is +0.0181, i.e. no reliable taxon-level ranking. BIO5 uniqueness is not identifiable after fully conditioning on correlated BIO1/BIO4 for many taxa. Do not equate coefficient units.
4. **Joint observability, not causation:** Of the 2,400 taxa with >=5 photographs in >=2 colour categories, 1,902 have geographic effects and 1,376 have thermal slopes, with intersection **1,070**. On that exact intersection, spatial mismatch difference is +2.82 percentage points (genus 95% +1.84 to +3.75), thermal white-fraction slope +1.22 points/index unit (+0.59 to +1.87). Both group means being positive does **not** establish positive cross-species covariance or mediation. A separate historical discovery/validation result associated photographed diversity D with geographic organization: 369 taxa rho +0.126637 p=0.007, 363 taxa rho +0.099288 p=0.025, not a 4,475-species refit.
5. **Alternative explanation and identifiability limits:** A stringent same-year/month, different-observer <=5-km matched null was unsupported (p=0.300 with >=5 pairs; p=0.275 with >=30 pairs). The repeated both-colour revisit signal was 12/38 windows versus null 14.82 (upper p=0.9107). A small AI organ review sensitivity moved a warm-minus-cold white-label contrast from +2.63 to +0.37 percentage points with a broad zero-spanning interval. These results block claims of locally maintained genetic morphs, stable species-specific climate responses and pigment fitness trade-offs.

## Required selection/transport audit before a stronger biological synthesis
Use the source registry as the **master index** (never replace NA with zero). For each estimator, create one row per source taxon with eligibility flag, explicit failure reason, photo-classification fraction, original photo count, observer count, 10-arcminute cell count, valid <=50-km pair count, source cohort, genus/family, photographed climatic variance, and geography span. Distinguish **structurally undefined** estimands (e.g., no nearby pair) from insufficient precision and failed classification. Record how many taxa fail each criterion, with nonexclusive reasons and an exclusive ordered first-failure reason.

For each P2 and shared-thermal estimate, report: (A) its natural eligible set, (B) intersection with repeated-colour support, (C) **exact same 1,070 species**, (D) identical photo/observer support restrictions, where computable. Report effect, uncertainty, sign fraction, and the natural-to-common difference with paired genus-bootstrap CI. **Do not compare p-values across different samples as evidence of biological change.** Refit from per-species outcomes; do not substitute published group means.

Fit a **selection model** predicting estimability from outcome-blind opportunity covariates (photo counts, classifiability, number of observers, cells, span, cohort, taxonomy); cross-validate by genus and report calibration and overlap. An inverse-probability-weighted sensitivity may address *sampling opportunity selection* only under measured-selection assumptions and positivity. It **cannot** recover an intrinsically undefined local pair estimand or prove missing-at-random classification. Include bounded MNAR sensitivity for missing colour labels and stratified taxonomic/cohort analyses. If positivity fails, restrict transport target to the empirical common-support population and label it.

Estimate cross-species coupling **only on paired species**: geographic effect vs thermal coefficient with source effort and climatic opportunity controls, whole-genus bootstrap, measurement-error/reliability sensitivity. Do not infer mediation or a single shared selection mechanism. Use a multilevel missing-outcome model only as exploratory, with observed-data likelihood per estimand, distinct measurement scales, and explicit sensitivity to missingness; never use it to impute biological zero.

## Proposed New Phytologist paper spine
**Title:** Geographic organization and heterogeneous thermal associations of photographed flower-colour variation within angiosperm species.

- Fig 1: original 4,475-species opportunity cascade with **non-nested** branches and a 1,070-species intersection; measured-photo examples including classification failures.
- Fig 2: P2 spatial similarity, coarse and nine-colour conditional nulls across spatial scales, with stricter <=5-km negative test.
- Fig 3: thermal white-fraction mean and full per-species distribution; two observer-disjoint means alongside near-zero species-specific slope reliability.
- Fig 4: paired **same-taxon** geography and thermal estimates (1,070) plus transport/selection sensitivity, once genuinely refitted.
- Fig 5 (optional): falsification/identifiability ladder: organ-stage review, missingness, revisit null, and testable field/genetic alternatives.

**Narrative:** Variation is observable across species; its photographic appearance is geographically organized; mean photographed white fractions are modestly higher along shared warm-site gradients, but species-specific responses are heterogeneous and unstable; geographic and climatic patterns can coexist without implying one inherited morph-maintenance mechanism. Unequal denominators reflect distinct observability conditions, which must be reported and audited, not concealed.

## Current paper in one sentence

A repeated-photograph, location-blind method allows **within-species** distributions of four visible flower-colour states and continuous nine-palette displacement to be measured across independently sampled plant species, revealing a recurrent **white–chromatic direction** and geographically allocated local mixtures/differences that motivate, but do not yet identify, evolutionary maintenance mechanisms.

The current paper is **not** the global 20,694-species congeneric flower-colour/rainfall source: that different macroecological estimand now lives in [mois](https://github.com/zuizui0223/mois). Do not append it as 20,694 within-species floral polymorphisms or a confirmed water-trade-off mechanism.

**Figure 1 and the integrated Table 1 now give the nonnested endpoint denominators explicitly.** Numbers cannot be added across rows, because eligibility differs by estimand. In particular, **439 is not 439 preregistered confirmed species**, and the 50km 166/181/204 subsets are selected by local photo-pair availability rather than a secondary W construction funnel.

The global photo colour response measured here is **visible image-derived flower-colour variation within named taxa**. It does not yet establish heritable stable colour-morph polymorphism *within natural reproductive populations*. Digital near-clipping also covaries with the frozen white category (OR 1.444, 95% CI 1.389–1.502); after a response-blind clipping exclusion the geometric W signal remained, but vector retention was **142/158=89.9%** and the frozen terminal technical gate is **INDETERMINATE**. These limitations are preserved in the manuscript and SI rather than silently overwritten.

## Main narrative and figure order

- **Method:** photographic flower-colour distribution D, observer-disjoint validation, fresh-image transfer, original colour measurement and ambiguity gates. **Figs 1–2**, Table 1.
- **Pattern:** nine-palette white–nonwhite W axis, discovered/validated in different species, then prospectively confirmed only in 158 pre-frozen new photo/species vectors. **Figs 3–4**.
- **Ecological location and interpretation:** local mixing and geographic differentiation (50km exact species-colour composition null), small and technically bounded BIO5-associated phenotype turnover, and separate hypotheses of pigment-network regulation, pollinators, water/light stress, dispersal, demography and drift. **Fig 5**.
- **Mechanism ceiling:** field genotypes, calibrated floral reflectance including UV, anthocyanin/pigment chemistry, water treatment response and colour-morph-specific relative reproductive fitness remain unmeasured. Proposed experiments, including a water-manipulation design, are **Discussion hypotheses**, not newly established empirical mechanisms. Interspecific mois regression outcomes are not transplanted as within-species maintenance coefficients.

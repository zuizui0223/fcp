# CHUN–FCP prospective spatiotemporal bridge opportunity — v0.1 result

## Decision

**HOLD_INSUFFICIENT_FRESH_CLADE_COVERAGE**

The bridge was qualified before any new colour, D, spatial-rho or cross-scale association was opened.

The frozen gate required:

- at least 5 unused U100 FCP species per temporal clade;
- at least 12 qualifying CHUN clades;
- at least 60 species in the deterministic future selection;
- maximum 10 selected species per clade.

The current untouched pool does not pass.

## Outcome-blind frame

The audit started from the frozen 3,230-species U100 third-cohort candidate frame, which already excludes the 1,000 legacy discovery/validation species and the earlier 500 P500 selections.

The 500 species already selected for the third prospective cohort were then removed.

This left **2,730 genuinely unused U100 species**.

Only exact first-token genus identity was used to map those species to the 28 CHUN common temporal clades. No synonym expansion, family fallback, flower-colour value, spatial result, CHUN effect size, geography or climate entered the mapping.

## Result

Among the 2,730 unused high-depth species:

- **127 species** belong to one of the 28 temporal clades;
- **10 clades** contain at least five unused species;
- the gate required **12 clades**.

The ten qualifying clades are:

- Cornus — 8
- Lonicera — 14
- Passiflora — 12
- Prunus — 16
- Rhus — 6
- Ribes — 11
- Rosa — 12
- Rubus — 8
- Solanum — 15
- Vaccinium — 9

Near-threshold but nonqualifying clades remain nonqualifying under the frozen rule: Cordia 4, Gaultheria 3, Scaevola 3, Capparis 2, Diospyros 2, Elaeocarpus 1 and Ilex 1.

## Why the threshold is not changed

Changing 12 clades to 10 after seeing these counts would turn the feasibility gate into a post-hoc rescue.

Likewise, broadening exact genus matching to families, synonyms or hand-selected neighbouring clades after this result is prohibited.

Therefore:

- no fresh bridge cohort is selected;
- no existing seven-clade validation correlation is calculated;
- no CHUN temporal effect values are opened for cross-scale model fitting;
- no biological inference is made from this HOLD.

## Interpretation

The temporal–spatial sister-paper symmetry remains supported as a **programme-level conceptual synthesis**, but the present FCP high-depth sampling frame does not contain enough independent temporal radiations to test direct coupling cleanly.

The next direct bridge requires genuinely new sampling availability or a separately designed harmonized dataset. It must not be obtained by relaxing this failed gate.

# FCP external flower-colour × independent preserved-specimen occurrences: frozen selection before GBIF outcomes

Status: **source-feasibility and taxon-manifest stage**. This research question follows retrospective FCP photography PRs #164, #188–190, and independent botanical-label-but-same-iNaturalist-site PR #191. It is **not** an independent prospective selection of region or hues. Only NHM strict species-level botanical flower-colour descriptions are used to choose candidate taxa. All statistical claims must retain the exact source, sample and geographical/support restrictions.

## Question and sources
Can the direction of the east 90–180°E precipitation association between botanical WHITE and BLUE/PURPLE species recur when **both** (a) flower-colour phenotype is taken from NHM text-extracted botanical descriptions rather than FCP photographic classification, and (b) occurrence sampling uses **GBIF PRESERVED_SPECIMEN** records, not FCP's original iNaturalist photo coordinates? Climate will be re-extracted at those independent specimen coordinates from the official WorldClim 2.1 1970–2000 normals (10 arc-minute BIO1, BIO5, BIO12, BIO15; elevation from official 10m grid). Historic trait source DOI `10.5519/p3dm31kc`, CC BY 4.0; version/bytes pinned to NaturalHistoryMuseum/plant-traits commit `4843c22bb2fc91018d9828dcddebf067c83b1097`, combined.csv, raw Git blob SHA1 `e1cfd2d5c518c2061ec91d1a9e8b64fc7ca69389`, 17,279,412 bytes.

Require >=30 source genera, >=3 source species per geographic side per genus, >=100 independently labelled species and >=20 of both hue categories in each side. ## New scientific independence and remaining risks
Independence is by **different source phenotype measurement** and **different primary occurrence record type**. It is *not* complete biological independence: some herbarium specimens may have shared observer/botanical collection biases, location errors and taxonomic synonym problems; the description source and GBIF database may both include historical herbarium collections. Source selection is deliberately biased towards genera with both nominally pure colours and cannot estimate plant-wide prevalence. Annual WorldClim rain is not actual microhabitat moisture or selection gradient. Negative evidence means no confirmed regional transfer on eligible sample, not no effect.

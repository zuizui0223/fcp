# FCP local morph recurrence, exploratory protocol (2026-10-08)

Question: Do white and coloured photographic morph labels reappear near the same coordinates across years and independent observers? This is not a genetic, fitness, or adaptation test.

Only measured observations with accepted visible colour labels are analyzed, with at least 40 classifiable photos/species. A comparison requires at least five white and five pooled nonwhite observations; a second comparison requires five each of two distinct nonwhite hues. Photo counts are not wild-population counts.

Three spatial scales: 10, 25, 50 km *maximum photo-pair diameter*. For each photograph anchor, collect observations within half the diameter. Strict local repetition requires >=3 photos per group, >=2 observed years per group, and >=2 distinct nonempty observers per group. Apply identical thresholds to (A) white + nonwhite and (B) two individual nonwhite hue classes. Missing years or observers cannot support the strict criteria. Report species as the independent units; overlapping anchors are not replicate populations.

For matched comparisons, only species eligible for BOTH kinds of global variation enter the paired 2 x 2 recurrence table. An exact discordant-pair test is retrospective and descriptive.

An additional temporal association contrasts white/nonwhite discordance among different-observer pairs in the same year versus across years, matching 0-10, 10-25, 25-50 km geographic bands and requiring ten pairs per year stratum. It does not estimate annual morph frequencies or changing fitness.

Limits: community-photo white labels have known exposure coupling. A repeated photo colour is not necessarily a pigment genotype; a 10-km neighborhood is not a confirmed mating population; repeated observations across years and observers are not proof of balancing selection, environmental adaptation, pigment biochemistry or pollinator selection. Keep all prior manuscript results frozen.

## Opportunity diagnosis and audit-only candidate names

A geographically maximin selection of 100 photos/species can leave very few locally repeated photographs even for genuinely polymorphic populations. Therefore a failure of the strict recurrence gate must not be interpreted as evidence of biological absence. Report separately the species with at least one neighbourhood of six photos irrespective of colour; and the stricter, colour-independent necessary condition of six valid dated, observer-labelled photos covering at least two years and observers. Divide strict white+nonwhite recurrence only by the number of white+nonwhite globally eligible species that also pass that independent neighbourhood opportunity condition. Even this is not full statistical detection correction because fixed colour compositions and observer/site selection can matter. Species-level examples from successful gates are an exploratory audit queue, not independent confirmation; no native-range or wild/cultivated status has been verified.

## Mechanistic falsifier: Moricandia arvensis known plastic flower phenotypes

Our outcome-derived recurrence candidate ledger contains iNaturalist taxon 165527, Moricandia arvensis, in the validation cohort. This species is a deliberately identified **positive control** for an important alternative explanation, not a new discovery: Gómez et al. 2020 (Nature Communications, DOI 10.1038/s41467-020-17875-1) showed that the same individual can bear purple/lilac spring flowers and white summer flowers. Gómez et al. 2024 (Evolution Letters, DOI 10.1093/evlett/qrae017) found that this floral plasticity itself can be maladaptive while selection for prolonged summer flowering maintains it indirectly. Thus repeated white/coloured *photographs* from one geographic neighbourhood are not evidence of balancing selection between genetically fixed morphs.

To audit whether the fixed 53-classifiable-photo validation subsample reflects this known direction, separately count photographed white and nonwhite states in March–May versus June–August at northern-hemisphere coordinates, report missing seasons, the signed summer-minus-spring white fraction and an exploratory one-sided Fisher diagnostic only when both seasons have at least five photos and both states at least three. Output exact month-level opportunity and the number of diameter-25km anchor windows with spring nonwhite and summer white photographs. Photography exposure and nonnative/garden status remain unknown; this is **not independent confirmation** of the field-identified mechanism, nor a representative test across species. If seasonal opportunity is absent, state not estimable rather than attributing a null to biology.

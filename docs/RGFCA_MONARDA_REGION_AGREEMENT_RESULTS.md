The single authorized 110-image run completed on **8 September 2026**, with
**110/110 alignment checks passed and zero model runtime failures**. It did
**not pass** the prospectively fixed localization-agreement gate. All rows,
including 15 empty predictions among 109 annotated images and one annotation-
unknown image, were retained. There was no second model run or threshold search.

The gate is conjunctive: passing recall does not compensate for either failed
precision floor. “Precision” here is the fraction of predicted pixels also
covered by supplied generic flower polygons; it is not verified petal purity.

or relabelled a verified negative. Provider splits remain descriptive only

and the best split was not selected.

## Scientific interpretation and next boundary

This is a completed **measurement-agreement failure under the declared
operational criteria**, not a technical execution failure and not a biological
absence result. The reference is a particular single-species, incompletely
characterized export with generic flower-region annotations, not verified focal
petals or exhaustive annotation truth. Low overlap can reflect localization
error, incomplete annotations or an ontology mismatch; this evaluation does
not distinguish those explanations or identify background pixels as its cause.
JRC object/box-derived floors are prespecified operational criteria, not
externally validated universal petal-pixel accuracy standards.

The weak reserve photo-level association remains recorded, and its matched
flower-minus-background differential remains unsupported (`p = 0.087`). This
additional localization gate does not rescue, erase or causally explain that
result. It further limits using the present atlas as verified floral phenotypes.

These 110 images are now outcome-opened. They must not become an “unused”
holdout or be repeatedly rescored to select a winning model. A future measurement
revision needs a separately justified target/annotation definition, recorded
development data and genuinely independent validation. Sharedness-v2 synthetic
geometry qualification is a different methods lane and cannot substitute for
floral measurement validity. The biological publication goal remains unmet;
the completed negative measurement evidence belongs in the methods/limitations
record, not in an unqualified ecological claim.

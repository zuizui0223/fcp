The first matched system reveals a particularly important biological-validation warning. The globally reused simple colour code treats yellow/orange as "nonwhite" and white as separate, but actual Dactylorhiza experimental colour polymorphism is yellow versus purple. Apparent white among 40 classified photographs may arise from pale yellow tissue, photography, other morphs or mistaken floral tissue. Without re-inspection it cannot be used as evidence of frequent white anthocyanin-loss alleles. This is a phenotype-identity warning, not a statistically verified misclassification rate.

### Why the mechanism evidence cannot be pooled across these systems

The underlying study hypotheses address **different biology**:

- Direct negative frequency dependence at manipulated colour frequencies (*Dactylorhiza sambucina*) — rare yellow/purple phenotypes receive pollination advantage. Does not require a universal metabolic cost of coloured petals. Original source DOI: 10.1073/pnas.111162598.
- Spatially variable ecology/selection on inherited bract morphology (*Castilleja coccinea*) — red/yellow colour varies with habitat, and common-garden red had lower fitness. This is not a white allele, nor proof of red-bract colour itself as the isolated selected component. DOI 10.1002/ajb2.70094.
- Phenological plasticity (*Moricandia arvensis*) — same organism can create lilac spring/white summer flowers; selection for seasonal reproductive timing need not select directly for the flower's visible colour. DOI 10.1093/evlett/qrae017.
- White-versus-pink multi-process advantage (*Silene littorea*), NOT present in any high-depth image cohort — pink has higher pollinator visitation, white can attain autonomous fruit/seed reproductive assurance, and two field populations show opposed *point estimates* of flower-number-mediated colour association with female fitness (Barra +0.418 p=.022 vs Melide -0.257 p=.064). The latter is marginal and the difference between coefficients was not tested for significance by this FCP program. Another source distinguishes petal-specific from whole-plant anthocyanin loss by chemistry; those are not equivalent white-morph genetic states. DOIs 10.3389/fpls.2020.588383, 10.1111/plb.13209, 10.1186/s12870-019-2082-6.
- Heritable but environment-inducible white versus purple floral colour with herbivore/abiotic fitness components (*Boechera stricta*), also absent from high-depth photo cohorts. DOI 10.1111/nph.14998.
- Drift and gene flow contributing to yellow/purple persistence in *Iris lutescens*, also absent. DOI 10.1093/aob/mcw036.

This source panel was PURPOSELY selected based on experimental/ecological knowledge rather than random screening. Its 3/11 photo taxon overlap is a **study-design feasibility result**, not an estimate of what percentage of all FCP species are balanced by frequency dependence versus other causes.

### Integrated FCP macroecology reading

Independent from this small organism-level panel, the large original photographic FCP study measured species-wide *sampled photographic ITV*:

- 50-km local colour discordance is lower than the same species' globally sampled photograph-composition null in 3 species-disjoint cohorts, even after month and year-month colour-count controls and independent-observer filtering.
- No universal signed latitude/elevation white/chromatic or four-state diversity band cline survives the 3-cohort support rules.
- No stronger unsigned white-versus-leading-nonwhite latitude association than a comparably measured leading-versus-second-nonwhite-hue association (32/34/41 matched species; paired signflip P=.6322/.7184/.5180).
- Broad photographic white/chromatic geometry remains a conditional source-system measured result, and near-highlight white classification coupling remains detected.

Therefore the current strongest ecological inference is **many species allocate photographed colour variation unevenly across space without a shared signed geographic direction**. The data do NOT yet estimate whether the allocation is caused chiefly by pigments' costs, herbivore protection, pollinator frequency response, autonomous selfing or limited migration.

### Priority mechanism questions, not retrospective claims

**First, origin/accessibility:** do true heritable colour changes repeatedly arise through floral-organ-specific pigment regulation rather than whole-plant loss? Independently assess petals, leaves and flavonoid biochemistry; photographs alone cannot decide PAL versus WAL.

**Second, persistence in one mating population:** where both genetically verified forms coexist, does relative seed/survival fitness reverse with morph frequency, pollination conditions, damage pressure or genotype? Distinguish local balancing selection from recurrent arrival of migrants/mutations or age-dependent pigment plasticity.

**Third, maintenance of species-wide geographic mosaic:** do independent, replicated field populations show different net colour-associated fitness signs, while accounting for neutral demographic structure, cross-pollination and selfing? Observational geographic photo clusters establish what needs explanation, not the selective force.

An important consequence is that a globally recurrent white/coloured photographic axis need not imply one universal cost–benefit balance. Systems can have a recurrent easy-to-switch pigment pathway but different local selective or demographic causes of persistence.

### Current claim boundary

No causal FCP trade-off meta-analysis is estimable from this source/photo crosswalk. Source-verified chemistry/individual genotype/fitness linkage count is zero. That is an identifiability limit, not evidence that flower colour has no fitness consequences, and it leaves existing FCP manuscript/other frozen primary tests unchanged.

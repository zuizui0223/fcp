# Floral-specific pigment decoupling coverage preflight — 2026-09-25

## Purpose

This is a coverage-only preflight for a future comparative test of the floral-specific pigment decoupling hypothesis.

The future outcome of interest is pleiotropic ecological/fitness cost. **No fitness, survival, reproduction, abiotic-stress or allele-frequency outcome is used to decide coverage in this preflight.**

## Unit

The unit is an independently characterized natural white-flower mechanism instance, not necessarily a species. Distinct loci within one natural species (for example the A and W loci of Ipomoea purpurea) may enter as separate mechanism instances.

## Eligible mechanism evidence

An instance is eligible only when all hold:
1. naturally occurring intraspecific white/pigmented floral variation;
2. a molecular mechanism is directly resolved or strongly functionally supported;
3. the mechanism can be assigned before reading pleiotropic-cost outcomes to one of the architecture classes below.

## Architecture classes

### DEC — floral/regulatory decoupling
Direct molecular evidence for a regulatory lesion, cis-regulatory change, or flower/petal-biased pathway reduction that can reduce floral pigmentation without an established whole-plant early-pathway knockout.

### SYS — systemic/extra-floral structural disruption
Direct loss-of-function in an anthocyanin structural enzyme with evidence that the affected pigment/pathway phenotype extends beyond petals to vegetative/stem tissue, or multi-tissue evidence inconsistent with a flower-restricted lesion.

### UNRESOLVED
Molecular white-state mechanism is known, but tissue scope or architecture is insufficient to distinguish DEC from SYS without using downstream fitness outcomes or speculative inference.

Candidate/regulatory-expression studies without a sufficiently resolved causal mechanism are retained separately and do not count toward the primary coverage gate.

## Frozen coverage gate

A comparative pleiotropic-cost test is authorized only if the molecular-only registry contains:
- >=5 strict DEC mechanism instances;
- >=5 strict SYS mechanism instances;
- >=3 plant families represented in each class.

The threshold is fixed before any new pleiotropic-cost literature is assembled.

If the gate fails, do not lower it. The hypothesis remains a mechanistic case-study model rather than a comparative test.

## Planned downstream test if coverage passes

Only after a PASS:
1. freeze the mechanism registry;
2. independently search/collate fitness and abiotic-stress evidence without changing architecture classes;
3. code whether each mechanism instance has documented extra-floral/reproductive/abiotic cost;
4. compare cost prevalence between DEC and SYS with family-aware uncertainty.

## Hard nonclaims

- Regulatory architecture is not assumed to be flower-specific unless direct evidence supports that interpretation.
- Structural mutations are not assumed systemic without extra-floral evidence.
- Absence of published pleiotropy is not coded as absence of pleiotropy.
- This preflight does not test the hypothesis; it only determines whether the literature supports a nontrivial comparative test.

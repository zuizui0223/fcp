## Single biological question and strict answer

**Question.** What is the global association of floral visible colour composition with moisture, which direction of colour variation repeatedly occurs within species, and could environmental differences in the relative benefits and costs of floral pigmentation explain either pattern?

**Current empirical answer.** (i) A broad but image-/geography-sensitive **between-species association** of photo-white colour with annual rain and vapour pressure is observed. (ii) A distinct high-depth, *species-disjoint prospective* comparison supports recurrent white-versus-coloured **colour-space direction** beyond a construction-preserving null, conditional on observed photographic classification. (iii) Near-half white in the photographed world is **descriptive, not statistically exceptional under the original species-conditioned latitude null**, and existing within-species, local-coexistence and geo-compensation studies do not identify a stabilizing frequency equilibrium. Pigment production costs, protective physiological benefits, pollen/seed fitness and negative frequency dependence were **not measured**. They are a future mechanistic hypothesis, not an inferred result.

## Paper reading order and manuscript constraints

1. **Observational moisture macroecology:** 20,694 climate-eligible species and soil-complete sensitivity; broad positive slopes but strict photo and spatial/external source limitations.
2. **Recurrent colour-space geometry:** distributional measurement + prospectively frozen 158-species achromatic–chromatic W result, not an unvalidated transition direction.
3. **What “balance” is not:** global classified photo white share ≈47%, source null for extraordinary regional constancy not supported, geographic offset null not supported, field/local intermediate rain coexistence not supported; distinguish photographic composition from within-population genetically polymorphic plants.
4. **Hypothesis and prediction:** environmental water balance may alter relative floral pigmentation benefit/cost through physiological protection, flower display and pollinator/florivore interactions. **A causal test requires the within-genotype/individual colour-morph relative seed/pollen fitness contrast under water manipulation, plus pigment chemistry and exposure control.** If fitness differences do not interact with water regime, this specific mechanism lacks support. A global 50:50 colour frequency is **not** a necessary prediction.

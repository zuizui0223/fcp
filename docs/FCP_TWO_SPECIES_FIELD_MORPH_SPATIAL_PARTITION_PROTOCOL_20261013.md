# Frozen before colour-clumping scores: two independent field datasets, nested morph structure

**2026-10-13 JST. Status: RETROSPECTIVE SOURCE-EXPOSED exploratory protocol.** Raw data were inspected for row grain, key integrity, and within-site colour totals **before** this protocol; the design is **not preregistered**. Do not claim independent biological confirmation of a new universal law or natural selection. Analysis follows this document without post-result hypothesis switching.

## Biological question
Can a field site contain both flower-colour forms while the morphs occupy **different local quadrats beyond the finite-sample clumping expected simply from plot sizes**? Does the same diagnostic show nontrivial differences in **two independently sampled plant taxa and studies**, without assuming their quadrats represent equivalent biological scales?

The question is explicitly **phenotypic spatial allocation at recorded quadrat resolution**, not stable persistence through time, locally balancing selection, individual plasticity, genetic divergence, or an environmental cause.

## Shared eligibility gate, fixed independent of clumping outcome
For each **observed within-species site-year** stratum (`year,population` for Sisyrinchium; `Site_name` for Boechera), define quadrats from unique source plot keys. For each quadrat, `n_j=purple_j+white_j` (Sisyrinchium) or `n_j=Boechera_flowered`, `purple_j=Boechera_pigmented` (Boechera); set `white_j=n_j-purple_j`. Retain **all** source quadrats in ledger. Exclude (n_j=0) only from within-M or within-Boechera colour-heterogeneity denominators; mark as `NO_TARGET_PLANTS`, not a white-morph quadrat.

Eligibility per stratum: (N=\sum_j n_j\ge20), at least 2 occupied quadrats, and at least 5 plants each of the two recorded colours at site-year scale. Monomorphic sites and insufficient sites get explicit **HOLD** (not zero clumping or evidence for random mixing). No filtering on observed clumping, null significance, environmental variables, density or direction.

## Exact opportunity-conditioned within-site null
For each eligible stratum with (V=\sum_jv_j\), (N=V+W), (m=#{j:n_j>0}), overall (p=V/N) and plot rates (p_j=v_j/n_j):

- (H_{between}=2\sum_j (n_j/N)(p_j-p)^2); (H_{total}=2p(1-p)).
- **Observed fractional between-quadrat colour diversity** (B=H_{between}/H_{total}=\sum_j n_j(p_j-p)^2/[Np(1-p)]). This is NOT population-genetic F_ST; it is a Gini-Simpson phenotype allocation on the **documented field plot** scale.
- Under the null that **individual colour labels are exchangeable among quadrats within the same site-year** while (N,V) and all (n_j) remain exactly fixed, the exact finite-population expectation is (E_0[B]=(m-1)/(N-1)). Its raw positive value is merely finite sampling, not biological clustering.
- Per-stratum effect (Delta=B-E_0[B]) in **percentage points of observed phenotypic diversity**. Primary species-level effect = arithmetic mean (Delta) giving **each eligible site-year equal weight**. Report observed mean (B), mean null expected (B), (Delta), the number and identity of eligible/excluded strata and all sample sizes.
- Fixed-plot-opportunity exact random-label null via **NumPy multivariate hypergeometric draws** conditioned independently on each stratum's fixed ((n_1,...,n_m),V). `R=9,999` replicates, seeds `2026101307` (Sisyrinchium) and `2026101308` (Boechera). Recompute species-level site-year equal (Delta) each draw, one-sided plus-one (p=(1+\#(T^*\ge T_{obs}))/(R+1)). This is a **fixed-site conditional random-label benchmark**, not randomized field placement, species-wide causal adaptation, or a phylogenetically independent study test. The user did not preregister these hypotheses.
- Descriptive (m_{mixed}=#{j:v_j>0\ &\ w_j>0}) and mixed fraction on **occupied** plots. Compute expected mixed count under exact multivariate hypergeometric null, plus Monte Carlo conditional lower-tail p **as secondary exploratory**, not replacement for primary variance effect.
- Sensitivity (pre-locked): on Sisyrinchium site names observed both in 2011 and 2013, retain **only the latest site-year** to avoid double-voting the same named population; exclude repeated earlier-year rows. On each species, require **min 3 occupied quadrats** as sensitivity. Report unconditional observations and sample support separately. Other-species counts L,S available only in Sisyrinchium are not part of the main null or plant abundance denominator and may be described in separate work, not added posthoc here.

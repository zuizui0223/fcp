## Why this changes interpretation of the adversarial colour-swap result

The separate 70 deliberately geographically optimized label-swap constructions in PR #150 included 64 species-pair swaps from species with fewer than 100 originally photographed 50km local pair edges. A few labels are mathematically influential in such low-edge networks because every photo can enter many local edges. But the genuine original colour spatial statistic remains positive with higher support (100+ edges) in all three species cohorts. Therefore the 0.3–0.55% targeted hypothetical label-swap fragility is **not by itself proof the observed spatial pattern is driven by low-coverage species or invalid photography**.

One-photo edge leverage drops in the median as the required local edge count increases, while photo-pair edges still share endpoints. For 100+ original near edges the median maximum incident edge proportion was 0.07048 / 0.07639 / 0.07258 in discovery/validation/third. Do not interpret these as independent photographed individual sample sizes.

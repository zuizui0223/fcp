# Cross-species causal evidence ladder for flower-colour geography
## 2026-10-08 — literature-informed ecology, separate from FCP macro estimates

### The scale of inference stays at 1,499 sampled species

The FCP New Phytologist mainline tests sampled intraspecific visible flower-colour variation using 149,900 photographs of 1,499 taxonomically distinct plants across three species-disjoint image resources, with 369/363/377 passing the primary D-evaluable gates. Its reproducible **species-balanced** ecological regularity is local depletion in colour differences at <=50 km in 166/181/204 species, with reported mean depletion 0.02053/0.01867/0.01468. This is one photometric system, and the selected high-depth species are not a probability sample of all angiosperms.

The added PR #129 test conditions this geographic organization on *each species' calendar-month colour composition*, including an independent-observer filter. This is still a photo-level inferential step, not causal identification of selection.

### Why a single mechanism is not supported by current ecology

The strongest ecological contribution of FCP is **recurrence of geographically allocated within-species variation without a universal signed colour–environment cline**. The observed positive geographic distance coefficient is **phenotypic IBD-like**, not proof of neutral drift or gene-flow limitation.

The best established counterexample is *Linanthus parryae*. Schemske and Bierzychudek (2007, Evolution, https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1558-5646.2007.00219.x) found a very sharp white/blue flower-colour boundary without a corresponding neutral allozyme cline, yet reciprocal transplants favoured the resident morph on opposite sides of a ravine. Geographic colour sorting can therefore reflect **divergent selection that does not track neutral genetic IBD**. The authors additionally stressed that colour itself versus pleiotropically associated traits remained unresolved; beetle pollinators did not appear to discriminate between morphs.

This matters for FCP's interpretation: a photo-derived phenotypic IBD-like pattern cannot be assigned to a single causal class even where geographic distance is statistically stronger than an environmental covariate.

### Independent study clusters and the causal stages they actually reach

| Independent organism/study cluster | Evidence available in primary publication | What it does NOT prove | Relation to large-scale FCP |
|---|---|---|---|
| *Linanthus parryae* (2007) | White/blue morph cline, allozyme comparison, reciprocal transplant and resident-morph seed-fitness benefit across environments | That pigment colour is the direct target, or that such selection dominates across species | Positive test case for local divergent selection even without a matching neutral marker cline |
| *Castilleja coccinea* (2025/2026) | Red/yellow bract colour heritable; habitat segregation; common-garden fitness contrast; morph/colour distance not correlated with geographic or genetic distance | Genetically proven pigment-target-specific adaptation in every population | Distinct selective landscape: spatial segregation of a bract colour phenotype not reducible to neutral IBD |
| *Leptosiphon parviflorus* (2025/2026) | Pink/white field morph frequencies at 21 sites and iNaturalist associations with serpentine soil, heat, water deficit and UV | Direct morph-by-habitat fitness crossover or an independently identified causal selective agent | Strong abiotic-sorting association, currently a lower causal stage than reciprocal fitness |
| *Abronia fragrans* (2025/2026) | Pink/white betalain phenotype; heritable pigment variation; transplanted plants show differing herbivory/florivory and spatial seed-set differences | That each local colour type has verified superior total fitness in its home habitat | Multi-agent trade-offs, not one temperature-to-colour direction |
| *Moricandia arvensis* (2024) | Same individual changes from lilac spring to white summer flowers; pollinator selection disfavours seasonal floral shift while summer flowering increases whole-plant reproductive output | Stable colour alleles or adaptive colour transformation | **Falsifies shortcut:** recurrent white+coloured photographs alone imply balanced genetic colour morphs |
| *Silene littorea* (2016/2019; source-derived FCP evidence) | HPLC distinction between petal-only and whole-plant anthocyanin loss; differential frequency and pathway expression | Equal mutation rates, a universal genome locus, or a direct molecular bridge to all 1,499 species | **Falsifies shortcut:** all apparently white flowers represent the same biological tissue-cost state |

### Exact source scope

- *Linanthus*: DOI 10.1111/j.1558-5646.2007.00219.x (transplant and neutral genetic controls).
- *Castilleja*: DOI 10.1002/ajb2.70094, PMID 40931921 (direct heritability, habitat, genetic and common-garden fitness).
- *Leptosiphon*: DOI 10.1002/ajb2.70018, PMID 40079352 (association, no reported morph-by-site fitness mechanism in abstract).
- *Abronia*: PMID 41408544 (herbivore/florivore outcomes, reproductive investment and site differences).
- *Moricandia*: DOI 10.1093/evlett/qrae017 (trait-specific maladaptive polyphenism and adaptive summer flowering).
- *Silene*: DOI 10.1186/s12870-019-2082-6 and current FCP secondary source ledger.

This is a **mechanism falsification set selected from published cases**, not a random six-species meta-analysis or independent replication of the FCP photograph cohorts. Morph identity, evidence type, and causal stage are intentionally heterogeneous; effect sizes must not be pooled as if they represented a common treatment/outcome.

### What biological generality would mean

The central, realistic cross-species hypothesis is *not* a universal temperature, latitude, or UV direction. It is:

**Ecological environments and history structure where floral colour variation occurs, while the causal mechanisms and even fitness sign of colour states can differ among species.**

This is presently a **working interpretation**, not a demonstrated universal evolutionary law. The large comparative photo dataset shows spatial structure; the separate literature cases establish that some mechanisms can generate it without demonstrating their prevalence.

A stronger claim needs at least three independent links:

1. Measurement validity: true reproductive-organ colour, not clipped white pixels or mixed organ/stage/sex categories.
2. Within-species eco-evolutionary state: verified local segregation versus genetic coexistence versus within-individual plasticity at real population scales; plus physiological/phenological state.
3. Selection/mechanism: colour-specific reproductive/survival fitness in contrasting environments **and** neutral population genetic or other historical controls, or direct pollinator/antagonist manipulations.

A future joint analysis can model whether environmental heterogeneity, dispersal structure or pollinator turnover predicts the **spatial allocation of ITV** across independent clades, but FCP's current photographic indicators by themselves do not identify fitness.

### Frozen inference boundaries for manuscript and public summaries

- Do not rewrite the positive historical D-local, H2, or other frozen paper results.
- Do not use the 42,111 opportunity frame as the prevalence denominator.
- Do not rename geographic phenotypic differentiation as confirmed within-population FCP.
- Do not claim geography being stronger than temperature supports *drift over adaptation*. *Linanthus* explicitly disproves that shortcut in one experimentally tested system.
- Do not assert white pigment absence from white-photographed petals, or protective benefit from visible hue alone.
- Do not use the mechanism examples to claim frequency of selection modes across angiosperms.
- If PR #129's monthly and observer robustness tests remain queued or fail coverage, report exactly that and retain existing replicated spatial result without upgraded claims.

## Interpretation and next action

The observed image diversity highlights why reliability of the frozen coarse colour classifier requires identification of the actual floral target (petal, bract, inflorescence, flowering stem, or ambiguous multiple plants), not only global image quality or location thinning. The full study's 50-km geography remains a result about observed photographed visible states, without demonstrated inherited plant flower-colour polymorphism, adaptive colour selection or reproductive fitness.

The next inferential action is independent blinded **human** annotation of the original photos, explicitly allowing 'not scorable' and mixed-organ cases.

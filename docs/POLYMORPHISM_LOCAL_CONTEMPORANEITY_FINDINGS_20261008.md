## Ecological interpretation and biological next step

- A rigid synchronous species-wide spring-coloured / summer-white dichotomy with identical photos and correct colour states would be inconsistent with strict same-year same-fortnight white+nonwhite co-observation. But asynchronous developmental/seasonal plasticity among individuals could produce this pattern without genetic polymorphism.
- For the three listed candidate taxa, the underlying image's actual flower structure and colour, phenological stage, cultivated/native-range context, and individual genotypes have not been independently revalidated. They are audit targets, not positive genotype controls.
- Therefore FCP's macroecological main inference remains species-wide colour variation is geographically partitioned more than exact within-species composition predicts. The 3/12 photo opportunities are too few to elevate a universal local balancing-selection or temporal plasticity law.
- The next genome/biochemistry/fitness stage needs actual wild-population cohorts with verified colour phenotypes and independent reproductive success, not a universal adaptation claim from photographed co-observation.

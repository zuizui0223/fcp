All notable changes to `disttrait` are recorded here while the package remains
inside the FCP monorepo.

- complete-row multivariate continuous-trait spatial inference;
- Euclidean pairwise trait-space distance;
- multivariate matched vertex-permutation nulls;
- 24-cell multivariate orientation-heterogeneity benchmark;
- exact optimization-equivalence guard for the multivariate null.

- frozen FCP/RGFCA algorithm-equivalence fixtures;
- scalar continuous-trait external transports;
- pooled-confounding, MCAR, MNAR, nonlinear and direction-heterogeneity stress tests;
- multivariate orientation benchmark with frozen receipt.
